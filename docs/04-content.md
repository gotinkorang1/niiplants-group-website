# 04 — Content Strategy

## Principle

Every sentence on the site should either state a fact or make a claim a
visitor can verify elsewhere on the page (a stat, a client name, a photo).
No line of copy should exist purely for tone. See voice rules in
[02-brand-guidelines.md](02-brand-guidelines.md).

**Vision** — draft:
"To be West Africa's most trusted diversified services group."

Home and About pages should present mission/vision/values only once
(About is the canonical location); Home references them in one short
strip, not a full restatement.

## Homepage content outline

1. **Hero** — group name, one-line positioning statement, single primary
   CTA ("Explore our companies") + secondary CTA ("About the Group").
   Full-bleed corporate photography (fleet, property, or operations —
   not a composited stock image).
2. **Group stats strip** — 3–4 numbers (`[X]` years, `[X]` sectors, `[X]`
   vehicles/properties, `[X]` locations) — only real, confirmed figures.
3. **Sector showcase** — grid of all 8 subsidiaries, each with name, a
   one-sentence descriptor (see below), and a link into that subsidiary's
   section/page.
4. **Why Niiplants Group** — 3 messaging-pillar callouts (Trust, Scale,
   Stability) backed by one concrete proof point each — not generic icon
   copy.
5. **Featured subsidiary spotlight** — rotating or editorially-chosen deep
   dive on one subsidiary with real photography, to prove depth behind the
   grid.
6. **Client / partner logos** (if available) or testimonial quotes —
   institutional credibility.
7. **News/insights teaser** — 2–3 latest items, links to full News page.
8. **Careers teaser** — one line + CTA to Careers.
9. **Contact / footer CTA** — group contact plus quick links to each
   subsidiary's own contact.

## Per-subsidiary content pattern

Each subsidiary page follows the same structure (see
[05-pages.md](05-pages.md) for the hub-and-spoke page spec, which differs
for the 5 companies with existing sites vs. the 3 that don't):
Hero → What we do (2–3 sentences) → Services/offering grid → Proof
(numbers/gallery/testimonial) → How to engage (visit external site / book /
quote / contact) → Cross-links to adjacent companies.

### Cross-linking logic

Because several companies are naturally adjacent, every subsidiary page
should surface 1–2 relevant sibling links in context, not as a generic
"other services" list:
- Niiplants and Car Rentals ↔ Plants Greene Leasing ↔ NPL Automobile
- Trivoxo (travel/tour) ↔ Plantsville Residences (stay once you arrive)
- Niiplants Logistics ↔ NPL Automobile (fleet servicing)

This reinforces the "one group, coherent portfolio" narrative from
[01-project-overview.md](01-project-overview.md) instead of presenting
eight unrelated business cards.

## Style guide for web copy

- Sentence case for headings (not Title Case), except the group/subsidiary
  proper nouns.
- Numerals for all figures ("8 sectors," not "eight sectors") in stats and
  data contexts; spelled out in flowing prose sentences.
- One CTA verb pattern per context: primary actions use imperative verbs
  ("Explore," "Request a quote," "Book now"); links to informational pages
  use plain nouns ("About the Group," "Careers").
- Dates in full (`14 March 2026`), not `14/03/26`, to avoid US/UK ambiguity.
- Currency in GHS with correct symbol (₵) where prices are shown; USD only
  if explicitly requested by client for an international audience.
