# 09 — Photography Shot List

A practical list anyone with a good phone can shoot in one or two days.
Every shot maps to a specific slot on the website. Shoot more than you
need — we'll select the best.

## How to shoot (applies to everything)

- **Landscape (horizontal) orientation** for all shots unless marked.
- **Highest resolution** your phone offers; no filters, no zoom (walk closer).
- **Daylight** — ideally early morning or late afternoon (soft shadows).
  Avoid harsh midday sun and dark interiors.
- **Clean the scene first**: move bins, cables, clutter out of frame.
  Wipe/wash vehicles before shooting.
- Hold the phone steady, tap to focus, take 3–4 of each shot.
- **People**: staff in neat work attire, doing their job naturally (not
  posed at the camera). Get verbal OK from anyone recognisable.
- Send originals (not WhatsApp-compressed) — use Google Drive or email
  attachments set to "actual size".

## Priority 2 — Per-company coverage

**Niiplants and Car Rentals** — key handover to a customer; driver in
neat attire opening a door for a passenger; airport pickup scene;
close-up of a clean dashboard/interior.

**Plants Greene Leasing** — a row of corporate vehicles; a lease
signing/handshake across a desk (faces optional); fleet keys on a board.

**NPL Automobile** — diagnostics laptop plugged into a car; engine bay
work close-up; suspension/brake work; the team standing in front of the
workshop (this doubles for Careers).

**Niiplants Logistics** — loading or unloading cargo; truck cab from a
low angle; warehouse shelving/pallets if available.

**Plantsville Residences** — every room type (wide corner shot from
doorway); bathroom (clean, dry, lights on); kitchen; compound/parking;
security post; laundry area.

**Trivoxo** — consultant with a client at a desk; passports/tickets on a
desk (no personal data readable); staff at work on screens (screens
angled away).

**Puffs Ghana** — 4–6 best dishes, shot from 45° near a window (natural
light is everything for food); the kitchen in action; a catering setup at
an event; storefront.

**Papersource** — stocked shelves; a prepared corporate order boxed for
delivery; storefront or office.

## Priority 3 — Group-level shots

- The whole team (per company or combined) — for About and Careers.
- Managers/leadership: individual head-and-shoulders portraits against a
  plain wall, landscape AND portrait versions — for the Leadership
  section (currently missing from the site because we have no photos).
- Any signage with company logos on buildings/vehicles.
- Hands-at-work close-ups (steering wheel, spanner, keyboard, serving a
  plate) — useful as section dividers.

I'll colour-grade for consistency, compress to web weight (fast loading,
no visible quality loss), then: replace the homepage hero gradient with
shot #1, add real heroes to each company page, build the Gallery section
from the brief, and add leadership portraits to About.
